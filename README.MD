INFO 2950 Project:

Phase 1:

Dataset Ideas:

1. We want to look at how different openings perform across different rating levels and time intervals.
   Further, we would most likely use either Chess.com's or Lichess.org's APIs in order to collect our data.
   They are both free and don't require an API key. Some downsides could be that our sample may not represent
   all players, and cheating or people with alt accounts could somewhat affect our results.
   Source links: https://www.chess.com/news/view/published-data-api, https://lichess.org/api

2. We also want to look at whether or not colleges with higher SAT scores have higher graduation rates using
   the College Scorecard API. This is free but requires an API key. We would compare a variety of different
   things including SAT scores, graduation rates, admission rates, and more. The U.S. Department of Education publishes
   this data, but could be missing scores and test submission policies could have an effect.
   Source links: https://collegescorecard.ed.gov/data/api-documentation

3. The last thing we thought about was whether or not playing a video game more means that people are more
   likely to recommend said game. We would collect reviews from a certain number of games through Steam's review API.
   We would compare positive and negative recommendations with playtime recorded when each review was posted.
   Steam directly provides these records, but idle time and differences between reviewers and other players could affect the results.
   Source links: https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/getreviews?l=english
